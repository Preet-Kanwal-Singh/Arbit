# Beta Instability vs. Engle-Granger Ordering (Tier B)

## Objective
To determine the sequencing of breakdown for the TCS/INFY pair following its established healthy cores. Specifically, we examine whether the empirical breakdown of the hedge ratio ($\beta$) precedes or follows the failure of the Engle-Granger (EG) cointegration test.

## Definitions
- **Source Data:** `rolling_metrics.csv` generated by Claim 002 (Codex).
- **Established Cores:**
  - `500d` Core: 2020-01-31 to 2021-12-31
  - `730d` Core: 2020-12-31 to 2023-03-31
- **$\beta$ Instability Onset:** The first month-end *after* the core end date where $\beta$ exits the $\pm 2$ standard deviation interval established solely during the core itself (i.e., $[\text{mean}_\beta - 2\text{std}_\beta, \text{mean}_\beta + 2\text{std}_\beta]$).
- **EG-Loss Onset:** The first month-end *after* the core end date where the Engle-Granger p-value $\ge 0.05$ (reusing pre-calculated anchors from Claim 003).

## Results

| Core | Mean $\beta$ | Std $\beta$ | $\beta$ Instability Onset | EG Loss Onset | Which First |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 500d | 0.681181 | 0.103646 | 2023-06-30 | 2022-01-31 | `eg_loss` |
| 730d | 0.670869 | 0.024954 | 2023-08-31 | 2023-04-28 | `eg_loss` |

## Analysis & Takeaways
For both the responsive (`500d`) and lagged (`730d`) windows, the statistical failure of cointegration (EG loss) significantly precedes the point at which $\beta$ itself exits its healthy empirical range.
- For the `500d` core, EG fails in January 2022, but $\beta$ remains within its $\pm 2$ Std Dev confidence bounds for another 17 months (until June 2023).
- For the `730d` core, EG fails in April 2023, while $\beta$ remains stable within bounds for another 4 months (until August 2023).

This indicates that spread predictability or mean-reversion characteristics (as measured by Engle-Granger) degrade far in advance of the raw hedge ratio itself structurally breaking down.
